Our Story

Built on care, grown by reputation

Obscura Lawn Care began with one truck, one mower, and one belief: that homeowners deserve a lawn company that actually shows up — and actually cares.

Fifteen years later, that belief still drives everything we do. We've grown into a full-service team of horticulturists, designers, and crew professionals, but we've never outgrown the personal, white-glove service our first clients fell in love with.

Today we care for thousands of lawns across Raleigh, Durham, Cary, and beyond — and we still treat every single one like it's the only one that matters.
