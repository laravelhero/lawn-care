A feeding program built for NC soil

The Piedmont's acidic clay soils and humid summers create unique challenges for turf. Our programs start with a soil test, then correct pH with lime when needed so the fertilizer you pay for is actually absorbed.

Timing is everything in North Carolina. We apply pre-emergents before soil temperatures trigger crabgrass germination, feed cool-season fescue in fall when it builds the most root mass, and tune nitrogen to avoid pushing disease in peak heat.

The payoff is a lawn that's noticeably thicker and greener within a season — dense enough to shade out most weeds naturally and tough enough to bounce back from Triangle summers.
