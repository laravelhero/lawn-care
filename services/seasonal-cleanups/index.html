Get your Triangle property season-ready

North Carolina's mature oaks, maples, and pines drop a heavy leaf load every fall — and leaving it on the lawn smothers turf, traps moisture, and invites disease. Our fall cleanups clear it completely, often across multiple visits as leaves continue to fall.

In spring, we clear the debris winter left behind, cut back spent perennials, sharpen bed edges, and lay fresh mulch so your landscape wakes up clean and ready to grow.

Serving Raleigh, Durham, Cary, and the surrounding Triangle, our crews come equipped for properties of every size — and everything we clear gets hauled away and responsibly disposed of.
